CASE FILE 001

The hook

Jo didn't wait to grow up.

Jo Nagai was 10 years old. Not a professor. Not a PhD student. A kid who raised caterpillars in his room and noticed something strange.

The butterflies he had raised would land calmly on his hand. Wild butterflies flew away the second he came close. Same species. Different behaviour. Why?

Most people would shrug. Jo wrote a four-page handwritten letter to a scientist and asked for help. Years later he stood in front of the International Congress of Entomology and presented findings that made a room full of experts go quiet.
