MYP Biology · Field Station Log #KX-19

Something surfaced from the vent. It's yours to figure out.

A submersible pulled an unclassified organism from a hydrothermal vent 2,400 m down. Unit 1 is your official classification report. Unit 2 is the field lab where you work out the chemistry keeping it alive. Pick a mission to begin — your progress is saved on this device.

For the teacher

This page runs entirely in the browser — no accounts, no server. Unit 1 is a formal, self-paced criterion assessment: students write extended responses, self-assess against the exact MYP Sciences strand descriptors, then print or copy a report for you to grade. Unit 2 is formative and ungraded by design — a set of gamified quizzes and two parameter-driven virtual labs (photosynthesis limiting factors; enzyme temperature/pH) that a class can run without physical apparatus, or alongside it. Publish this file as-is on EdgeOne (or any static host) — everything is a single self-contained HTML file.
